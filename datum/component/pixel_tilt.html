pixel_tilt
/datum/component/pixel_tilt

Allows living mobs to visually tilt in place while preserving unrelated transform changes.

Vars

last_applied_tilt_angleLast tilt angle applied to the mob's transform, used to undo only our own rotation.
maximum_tiltMaximum allowed tilt angle in either direction.
tilt_angleCurrent tilt angle being requested by the player.
tilt_incrementAmount of tilt added or removed per key press.

Procs

apply_tiltApplies or updates the visual tilt rotation based on player input.
reset_tilt_and_removeRemoves the currently applied tilt rotation from the mob's transform, then deletes the component.
snap_tilt_angleSnaps internal tilt angle values to fixed precision to avoid floating point drift.
