radioactive_contamination
/datum/component/radioactive_contamination

Surface radioactive contamination.

This represents radioactive dust or residue stuck to an atom. It is separate from /datum/component/irradiated: contamination can sit on objects, turfs, or mobs, can be washed off, and only primary contamination can rub off further.
